Pine & Palm Golf

How to play

Hold the left mouse button, pull the mouse straight back toward you, then push it straight forward through where you started. How far back you pull sets the distance: with a normal push forward, half a backswing carries about half as far (the blue dot on the Backswing meter shows about how far back for the pin). A slow push loses distance; pulling back past full adds power you can't control. Push up and a little to the right for a draw, up and a little to the left for a fade; the more you slant it, the more it bends, until it hooks or slices. Backspin comes from your follow-through: stop short and sharp just past where you started for more backspin, or keep pushing through for a long follow-through and the ball comes off with less spin and runs. The scroll wheel adds or takes off loft. Arrow keys aim and change clubs. On a phone, swing with your finger the same way: touch, pull down, push up through where you started. The buttons at the bottom left change the club and loft, and hold ◀ or ▶ to aim (a tap nudges it).

End this round?
